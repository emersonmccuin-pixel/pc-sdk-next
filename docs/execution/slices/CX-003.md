# CX-003: Codex native-execution safety decision

Status: accepted decision — the product owner explicitly approved ADR-0002 as proposed
after three independent exact-hash hostile reviews reported no P0/P1/P2. Guarded
decision closeout remains in progress

## Objective

Produce the evidence-backed product and toolchain decision required before a
real Codex app-server process, thread, or turn can enter PC-SDK. Close two
separate pre-native questions without weakening either one:

1. how the local server can atomically contain the app-server process and its
   ordinary in-tree `CreateProcess` descendants on Windows 11 25H2 x64 client
   build `10.0.26200`, including after a hard server kill; and
2. how a stable app-server can positively attest the exact closed-world
   effective external-action, sandbox, and approval policy before the first
   turn.

CX-003 is decision-only. It may inspect checked-in schemas, pinned upstream
source, primary platform documentation, and local build-tool availability. It
must not implement or compose a production native peer, start Codex, touch a
credential home, or convert a missing policy receipt into inferred safety.

## Required evidence standard

A production admission receipt must contain provider-originated effective-
policy facts that PC-SDK independently verifies and atomically joins to its
immutable selection and attempt before anything model-visible or side-effect-
capable starts. At a minimum it needs:

- pinned runtime/protocol/schema identity and the complete immutable PC-SDK
  selection: runtime, account, model, and effort;
- exact effective provider auth identity, native account/workspace, auth mode,
  plan, service tier, and subscription-versus-API billing route;
- exact continuation-attempt identity, create/resume mode, requested native
  thread id or explicit absence for create, canonical cwd, and closed request/
  notification vocabulary;
- a closed-world inventory and provenance for every enabled model-visible tool
  and runtime-initiated external-action source: built-in native, dynamic/app-
  defined, PC-SDK bridge, exact effective MCP server identity/config/provenance/
  connection state plus tools/resources/resource templates/prompts/instructions,
  apps/plugins/skills/deferred tool namespaces, hooks/notifiers, collaboration/
  subagents/code mode/remote control, companion hosts, and external process
  brokers;
- exact sandbox, filesystem, and network policy;
- exact approval policy/reviewer, with `approvalRequests: disabled` for the
  initial empty-capability gate, no auto-reviewer/guardian/escalation fallback,
  and deny-on-unapproved-or-unknown semantics for every gate;
- an effective-configuration provenance digest and immutable policy epoch;
- atomic binding of all those facts and the policy digest to the exact
  selection, attempt, and returned thread; and
- a quiescent admission barrier that gives PC-SDK time to verify and explicitly
  accept the receipt before any external-action source, turn, or model work can
  initialize.

Two future stable wire shapes can satisfy the barrier:

1. a passive preflight binds PC-SDK's fresh challenge to the complete receipt
   and a single-use token; after PC-SDK verifies it, `thread/start` or
   `thread/resume` consumes that exact token and echoes the binding before
   initialization; or
2. `thread/start` or `thread/resume` creates only a quarantined thread and
   returns the challenge-bound complete receipt. The runtime guarantees that
   no policy-relevant source, turn, or model work can initialize until PC-SDK
   verifies the receipt and sends a positive admission commit bound to its
   thread id, attempt id, policy epoch, and digest. Rejection, timeout, or lost
   connection disposes the quarantine without activation.

A one-phase response is insufficient because the runtime could initialize an
action source while PC-SDK is still verifying it. After admission, the policy
epoch is immutable for the admitted attempt and turn. Turn-policy overrides and
settings/catalog/skill/app/MCP/hook/sandbox/approval changes cannot take effect
until the runtime reaches a quiescent barrier and PC-SDK independently admits a
new complete receipt. Model reroute is selection drift and follows the stricter
new-session rule below. Unknown or unlisted actions always deny. Session-wide
approvals and exec/network policy amendments remain forbidden unless a later
explicit contract and newly admitted receipt authorize them.

Effective provider auth identity, native account/workspace, auth mode, plan,
service tier, billing route, runtime, model, or effort cannot change inside an
app session. Any change or uncertainty fences the active attempt and requires a
new immutable PC-SDK selection/session plus full admission. Credential/token
refresh may continue only after positive proof that identity, subscription
route, and all those fields are unchanged; a notification after drift is not a
preventive receipt.

Configuration input, a layered config read, a negative feature list,
`approvalPolicy: never`, `sandbox: read-only`, a refused server request, or a
post-start item notification is not that receipt. Unknown, incomplete, stale,
or unbound evidence is `unavailable` and refuses native execution.

## Containment invariant

PC-SDK owns two narrow native owner-TCB artifacts: a transient cold-start/controlled-
restart bootstrap launcher and the privileged launcher/attester addon. The bootstrap is OS-loaded
from a qualification-sealed code-integrity rule and owner-nonwritable protected
install/namespace/postmortem-policy baseline. Its externally invoked `entry-scrub`
mode is never admitted owner authority: it cannot acquire SF-001 or open product state.
Every transient mode (`entry-scrub`, `cold-bootstrap`, and `controlled-restart`) arms,
as its first fallible action, an independently progressing native watchdog thread with
an explicit restrictive noninheritable descriptor and sealed mode-specific hard
self-deadline. Watchdog creation/query failure immediately fails the mode. It accepts
no external input and has no cancel/reset/extend path; process exit is its only disarm.
It may observe only sealed monotonic cleanup-state publication that cannot move its
deadline. Because
a watchdog cannot cover its own creation/arm call, scrub retains an exact query/
terminate/synchronize lifecycle handle for `cold-bootstrap` while its sole outer-job
source handle also makes scrub death kill the child, and old owner similarly retains
controlled-restart bootstrap while the unchanged outer job covers owner death. A live
N7 launcher may retain the same least lifecycle handle for responsive timeout/
terminate/wait, but closing a process handle does not terminate its process. N7 death
or hard kill after create and before positive scrub-watchdog arm can therefore orphan a
secret-free scrub. That outermost create-return/publication/pre-arm race is an explicit
unresolved production blocker. The separate N7 launcher-lifecycle decision must supply
a crash-race-safe kernel anchor plus exact death/hard-kill/handoff receipt and may
change the outer-job ownership topology. CX-004 does not qualify or freeze that edge.

After positive watchdog arm, entry-scrub's main path creates/configures/queries the
permanent unnamed kill-on-close outer owner job, including exact zero
`JobObjectBasicUIRestrictions` so later nesting is legal. It immediately sets/queries
source noninherit plus `HANDLE_FLAG_PROTECT_FROM_CLOSE`. The scrub process is its sole
holder; the main path atomically publishes the exact handle/configuration state to the watcher

terminates scrub. Thus an injected hang in job create/configure/query/publication is

   quarantine, publishes nothing, terminates the owned leaf with exact-root fallback,
   restores parent baselines, and mandates the poisoned non-restart shutdown path.
   Only after positive close may the addon atomically publish the immutable spawn receipt
   and retained ownership of the newly owned lifecycle-job, least root-process lifecycle,
   and I/O handles
   for the complete peer lifetime; a provider session also retains its separate assign-
   only handle. The coordinator releases only after that publication.

Any pre-resume create, attribute, query, assignment, nested-job, or handle
uncertainty terminates only the newly owned immediate leaf job, uses exact-root
termination as an additional fallback, and returns typed
unavailability. Temporary child-handle inheritance uncertainty additionally
poisons the process-creation coordinator as above; job termination cannot repair
a possibly inheritable handle retained by the parent. Any `ResumeThread` result
other than exactly one makes execution state unknown; it never assumes
   suspension and follows the same owned-leaf termination path. Any job/process/thread
   target derivation failure occurs before a broad-source close attempt, so every
   untouched broad source remains known cleanup authority. If all targets derive but a
   broad job/process/thread close is nonpositive or uncertain, that attempted value is
   quarantined permanently; no resume occurs, and cleanup uses only independently known
   derived handles, untouched broad sources, and exact-root fallback. Every case remains
   pre-resume. Before ordinary failure cleanup completes, the addon waits for the root to signal and the owned leaf to reach
zero/empty membership, then proves every borrowed explicit and implicit ancestor has
returned to its exact pre-create baseline. It never calls `TerminateJobObject` on or
closes a borrowed owner/session handle. Failure to prove removal poisons the
coordinator and escalates to durable attention plus non-restart owner shutdown; it
does not kill a borrowed parent in place. RAII unwind deletes attribute-list storage
and closes every positively still-owned temporary, thread, process, and I/O handle on
every spawn-failure path; it never touches a close-outcome-quarantined numeric value.
For derivation failures it closes only known-live values in manifest order; for the job
object, any derived assign-only handle precedes untouched broad source and job lifecycle
last when present, or broad job source last when lifecycle derivation failed. It also
closes known broad/derived process and thread handles without using a quarantined value.
Kill-on-close is a final best-effort action only when every other owned handle for that
job is positively retired. After any source-close uncertainty RAII makes no last-handle
claim and leaves that quarantined source value untouched. Borrowed handles remain with their owning session/coordinator. Any owned-
resource release failure or uncertainty poisons the coordinator, requires non-restart
owner shutdown, and cannot report cleanup; process exit is the final fail-safe for
process-owned raw OS/native resource cleanup but never proves a quarantined release.
On every failure the coordinator remains held through terminate/wait,
leaf-zero and parent-baseline proof, ordered known-resource cleanup/quarantine, and atomic
poison/unavailability publication; only then may it release.
`CREATE_BREAKAWAY_FROM_JOB` is never used.

After resume, a hostile root can create an unnamed inner job and assign itself using
its pseudo self handle. PC-SDK cannot enumerate or recover that unnamed immediate-job
handle from outside, so post-resume receipts claim only exact membership and complete
PID lists for the retained PC-SDK-owned ancestor/leaf jobs—not a total immutable job
chain. This self-nesting is an explicit non-escape/denial-of-service residual: it cannot
remove the process from any no-breakaway PC-SDK ancestor, and terminating the owned
leaf still kills it and its descendants. CX-004 must prove that canary non-vacuously;
CX-005/008 separately deny cross-process assignment/handle effects. Any design that
needs to forbid self-nesting requires a new enforceable OS boundary and decision.

These ordinary addon leaf rules do not govern the separately sealed bootstrap
protocols. Scrub→cold alone explicitly assigns the permanent outer job. Cold→Node
inherits it and, before owner resume, proves the exact transitional outer membership
`[coldBootstrap,owner]`; after witness/job handoff, source close, and exact bootstrap
exit, the admitted owner proves steady membership `[owner]`. Controlled restart proves
each separately specified old-owner/bootstrap/new-owner transition. Uncertainty in
those specialized protocols may terminate the permanent outer job as defined above,
but no generic leaf RAII path explicitly closes its protected process-lifetime handle.

Normal provider-session disposal first stops new action admission under the process-
creation coordinator, settles every in-flight action create, proves every action job
disposed and no remaining assign-handle use, then positively closes the owned session
assign-only handle. An uncertain close poisons the coordinator, mandates non-restart
owner shutdown, and cannot report cleanup. Normal explicit disposal may then request
graceful provider shutdown in a later contract, but if the tree remains it must call
`TerminateJobObject` through the newly owned lifecycle handle, not invent
a POSIX signal. `terminationMode` records `natural`, `graceful`, or `job` as an
action/outcome attribute; it is not cleanup proof. The addon retains that lifecycle
handle while querying the active count and complete process-id list to zero,
captures the exact root exit code, and observes the root handle signaled and
stdio EOF, then proves every borrowed/implicit parent has returned to its exact
pre-create baseline. Only then may `cleanupProof: tree-exited` authorize success. A
timeout or query failure sets `cleanupProof: uncertain`, closes every remaining known-
live owned I/O/process handle and the owned lifecycle handle last, leaves borrowed handles
with their owners, and never claims success. A provider-session assign handle was
already positively closed before this phase; every other leaf never retained one.
Any close uncertainty follows the poison/non-restart shutdown rule rather than
claiming no leak. Disposal with
pending I/O atomically stops new input but retains
all native state until every outstanding read/write/cancellation settles exactly
once. It cannot block the Node event loop, free state before a completion, emit
late/double callbacks or bytes after settlement, or report cleanup while native
I/O requests or worker threads remain.
Completion-port events are diagnostics, not sole proof, because their delivery
is not generally guaranteed. Crash containment is proven from an external
harness. Immediately before the kill, the witness must prove the root and every
deterministic descendant handle are unsignaled, fixture enumeration is
complete, and a heartbeat/marker is advancing. Without holding the job handle,
it hard-kills only the server owner, requires every retained process handle to
transition to signaled within a bound, and proves no marker advances afterward.
The dead server cannot mint its own receipt. This CX-004 witness proves the
declared deterministic fixture did not inherit the job handle; it does not claim
that a same-user hostile descendant cannot open the owner with
`PROCESS_DUP_HANDLE`, duplicate/retain/transfer/close a job handle, or otherwise
keep the last job handle alive. Nor does it deny opening an out-of-boundary
process with `PROCESS_CREATE_PROCESS` and using
`PROC_THREAD_ATTRIBUTE_PARENT_PROCESS` to inherit that process's job state.
CX-004 does not prove those OS-enforced denials. CX-005 must prove every provider-
root-to-owner/outside direction before credentials; CX-008 revalidates the root
proof and adds every lower/sibling/cross-tier direction.

The declared hostile same-user scope begins with PC-SDK-created provider/lower/
transition/Git descendants; it does not claim resistance to arbitrary pre-existing
same-user malware racing bootstrap or owner startup. If that broader product threat
is later selected, production requires a separately approved OS-trusted pre-first-
instruction launch anchor. Protected-install/code-integrity evidence or a runtime
self-DACL change cannot substitute for that anchor.

The admitted native owner/runtime process boundary begins at `entry-scrub`. The
external one-click UI launcher and browser activation remain N7 operations work under
`docs/agent-runtime-architecture.md`; the current VBS→PowerShell launcher and its
PowerShell `Start-Process` server/browser paths are not full-spawn-qualified and are
non-admitted as production server boot. CX-003 constrains the routine-path minimum for
a future N7 server edge: invoke exact `entry-scrub`, pass no authority or inherited
handle, derive/retain only an exact query/terminate/synchronize scrub lifecycle handle,
require exact success or terminate/wait on a sealed timeout, then close it. That process
handle is insufficient for N7 crash/hard-kill. Production requires the separate N7

watchdog arm, exact death/hard-kill proof, and any resulting job-handoff topology.
Browser activation is not assigned to the owner job and is not represented as an owner
descendant. CX-004 does not qualify that N7 edge, must not freeze its topology, and does
not implement or qualify the UI launcher/browser boundary.

  There is one launch topology
  with no runtime fallback: the in-process PC-SDK launcher/attester directly
  creates every action root. No persistent lower protocol/tool/MCP router is
  admitted. A future need requires a new product/security decision, its own atomic
  pre-first-instruction restricted receipt/lifecycle, and proof that it holds no
  effect, launch, job, attester, credential, or ambient IPC authority.
  Every effect capability is a disposable call-bound lease. The initial topology
  is exactly one PC-SDK app session to one provider root and one outer session job;
  every thread/turn on that root shares a durable action queue that permits at most
  one effect lease/action tree. A new action root in that session job may start only after
  its prior action has positive cleanup, handle closure, lease revocation, and
  post-state proof. The queue identity/cardinality are in composite and per-call
  receipts. Parallel app sessions/builders use distinct roots/jobs, disjoint run-
  owned worktrees, and the global canonical-target exclusion below; every cross-
  session action principal is a protected sibling. Every process in any future
  process-capable tree still requires its own independent pre-first-instruction
  receipt and remains part of the one receipted action tree; job membership or
  inheritance alone never admits a descendant. Reusing one root/job across app
  sessions or concurrent independent roots in one session needs a new decision
  and proof.

  Here `1:1` means one active provider deployment generation at a time, not one
  root for the app session's lifetime. Each replacement root/session job receives a
  durable monotonic generation and fresh CX-005 root receipt; after CX-008 it also
  requires fresh deployment/provider/composite admission before activation. It may
  start only after positive prior-generation action/session-job zero, I/O/handle
  closure, lease/resource recovery, and no-overlap proof; uncertainty quarantines
  the old generation and blocks replacement. Resuming the same native thread also
  requires positive adapter continuation capability plus a fresh provider policy
  epoch bound to the new process generation. No old receipt admits a replacement,
  and old/new roots never overlap. Reusing one live root across app sessions or
  running concurrent root generations remains forbidden.
